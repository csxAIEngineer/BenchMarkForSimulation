### 横财应酬

In the past two weeks, a lottery prize this person had already claimed was paid into the account. The money was already there. This person was at home when it arrived. The days that were already on the calendar were dropped. Relatives and strangers kept calling this person to come sit with them. This person now goes to those gatherings and stays through them, instead of keeping the day that had been planned.

### 远处爆炸

In the past two weeks, a nuclear explosion far from this person had already ended. This person was outside the blast. This person was at home when the news arrived. Since then this person still startles at a loud sound, still checks the window before stepping out, and still leaves a room when the noise keeps up.

### 当众说完

In the past two weeks, while this person was at home, this person stood where others could hear and finished saying a matter that had been held back, through to the last sentence. Since then, when people are gathered and a point is this person's to give, this person says it through to the end instead of stopping after the opening line.

### 决裂

In the past two weeks, while this person was at home, this person and someone close parted. The parting is already done. This person no longer calls that person, no longer answers that person, and no longer goes to the places where they used to meet.

### 新任务

In the past two weeks, while this person was at home, this person took up shaping small figures out of clay, which this person had never done. This person now sits down to that clay when the evening comes. That sitting was not part of the days before.
